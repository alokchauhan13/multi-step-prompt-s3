2. Peak Distraction Times
What: Analyze timestamps of non-work intents (relaxation, curiosity, habit).

Why: Identify when the user is most prone to distraction.

Insight Example: “You tend to drift into habit browsing around 2 PM.”

3. Intent Switching Patterns
What: Sequence analysis of browsing intent (e.g., work → curiosity → habit).

Why: Understand how users transition between focused and distracted states.

Insight Example: “Most work sessions end with curiosity-driven browsing within 25 minutes.”

4. Productivity Ratios
What: Compute ratio of work vs. non-work (relaxation, curiosity, habit).

Why: Track effectiveness and improvement over time.

Insight Example: “Your work focus improved by 15% compared to last week.”

5. Top Distraction Sources
What: Identify top websites visited under distraction intents.

Why: Highlight specific distractions (e.g., YouTube, Reddit).

Insight Example: “Reddit accounted for 42% of your curiosity-driven visits.”

6. Intent Drift Detection
What: Detect when a work session gradually turns into relaxation/habit browsing.

Why: Reveal how attention erodes during sessions.

Insight Example: “Your average drift from work to relaxation takes ~12 minutes.”

7. Goal Alignment Score
What: Match visits and intent against user-defined daily goals.

Why: Score how aligned browsing behavior is with planned focus.

Insight Example: “Today’s focus score: 78%. You stayed on task until 4 PM.”

8. Session Clustering
What: Group sessions based on sequence, duration, and intent transitions.

Why: Discover behavior patterns (e.g., "deep work sessions", "impulse scrolls").

Insight Example: “You average 3 deep work blocks per day lasting 45 mins.”

9. Daily/Weekly Trends
What: Analyze intent breakdown across days and times of day.

Why: Reveal routine patterns and optimal focus periods.

Insight Example: “Mornings are your strongest work hours. Afternoons see a rise in habit visits.”

10. Habitual Site Classification
What: Identify frequently visited sites that fall under “habit” or “curiosity”.

Why: Suggest blocks or time-boxing strategies.

Insight Example: “Twitter was visited 18 times today, usually out of habit.”
